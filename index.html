Fox Sports Performance

Take your game further.

Led by Bend FC soccer coach Jackson Fox, private athletic training helps athletes build strength, develop speed, and move with confidence.

Gym training at Bend Biomechanics

Functional strength and movement work in the gym, so you're stronger, more durable, and better balanced on the field.

Speed training outdoors

Acceleration, agility, and change of direction on the Bend FC turf fields at Pine Nursery Park and the track at Central Oregon Community College.

Workshops

Two weekly workshops, each one hour. Drop in to a single session or lock in your day with a season pass.

01 · Speed

Athletic Speed & Agility Workshop

Footwork, acceleration, top-end speed, and change of direction. Held on the Bend FC turf fields at Pine Nursery Park and the COCC track.

Wednesdays & Sundays · from $20

02 · Strength

Functional Biomechanics Workshop

Gym-based strength and movement training at Bend Biomechanics, built to carry over to competition.

Mondays & Thursdays · from $20
